## Excluded assets

The private working project's dental chair, plant, Hertford chair, U table,
grand fir, reception desk, iMac and model 222 are not distributed here.
Their permissions were either royalty-free with redistribution constraints or
not recorded. Neither conversion to GLB nor this project's MIT license grants
additional redistribution rights. Download and import your own authorized assets.

Python, Blender and Windows fonts are external prerequisites and are not bundled.
Windows font outlines are generated locally from installed fonts; no font files
or pre-generated font meshes are included in this archive.
